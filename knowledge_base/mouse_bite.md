# Mouse bite

## Description
Small notches or bites along the edge of a copper trace or board edge, leaving a reduced trace width.

## Likely causes
Imperfect etching, depaneling stress at the board edge, or damage from routing and handling.

## Severity
Medium. A shallow bite may be acceptable, but a deep bite narrows the conductor and can cause an open circuit under load.

## Recommended action
Measure the remaining trace width against the specification. If below the allowed minimum, reject the board. Review etching and depaneling settings if the defect repeats.
