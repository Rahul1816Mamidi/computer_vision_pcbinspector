# Spur

## Description
A thin spike of copper sticking out from a trace, narrowing the spacing to a nearby conductor.

## Likely causes
Incomplete etching at a resist imperfection or contamination during exposure.

## Severity
Medium. It reduces electrical clearance and can become a short under vibration or high voltage.

## Recommended action
Measure the remaining clearance to the nearest conductor. Rework by careful removal if allowed, otherwise reject. Track frequency by panel to spot process drift.
