# Spurious copper

## Description
An isolated blob or island of leftover copper in an area that should be bare laminate.

## Likely causes
Under-etching, contamination, or resist defects that left copper unetched.

## Severity
Low to medium if isolated, high if it sits close to conductors and could bridge them.

## Recommended action
Check its distance to neighbouring traces. Remove or accept according to the specification. Review the etch process if such blobs appear on multiple boards.
