# General inspection policy

## Verdict rules
A board is marked FAIL when at least one defect is confirmed (detector confidence at or above the gate, or confirmed by the visual second opinion). A board is marked REVIEW when only low-confidence or disputed detections exist and a human should look at it. A board is marked PASS when nothing was detected.

## Escalation
Any short or open_circuit on a production board should be treated as blocking. Cosmetic defects such as small spurs can be reworked or accepted depending on the customer specification.

## Limits of automatic inspection
This guidance is general and educational. It does not replace the customer specification or a formal workmanship standard. Automatic detections can be wrong, so low-confidence findings must be confirmed by a person.
