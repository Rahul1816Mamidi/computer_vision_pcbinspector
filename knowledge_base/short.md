# Short circuit

## Description
Unwanted copper connects two traces or pads that should be isolated.

## Likely causes
Under-etching, residual copper, resist defects, or conductive debris between conductors.

## Severity
Critical. Shorts can damage components and the assembled product.

## Recommended action
Reject or send to rework where a controlled repair is allowed. Inspect the etching line and stripping stage, and check other boards from the same panel.
