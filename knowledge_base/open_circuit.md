# Open circuit

## Description
A copper trace is broken, so current cannot flow along the intended path.

## Likely causes
Over-etching, a scratch or handling damage, dust or resist defects during exposure, or a lifted trace.

## Severity
Critical. The circuit will not work as designed.

## Recommended action
Reject the board unless the customer specification permits a wire repair. Check the etching process and photomask for repeating defects.
